How it works

This converter matches your horizontal 360° turn distance (cm/360) between games, based on the actual OpenMoHAA engine source code.

MOHAA sensitivity formula (from CL_MouseMove and CG_CalcFov):

  • degrees_per_count = sensitivity × m_yaw × (fov_y / 75)
  • fov_y = 2 × atan(0.75 × tan(cg_fov / 2))
  • cg_fov is horizontal FOV at 4:3. The engine uses Hor+ scaling, so fov_y (and sensitivity) stays the same at any aspect ratio or resolution.

CS: Uses a base yaw of 0.022 and scales custom FOV relative to the default 90°. degrees_per_count = sensitivity × 0.022 × (FOV / 90). This assumes zoom_sensitivity_ratio 1; FOV 90 leaves the sensitivity unchanged.

V/H ratio bypass: If your MOHAA m_pitch differs from m_yaw, the vertical/horizontal sensitivity ratio won't match in CS (which has m_pitch locked at 0.022). The converter adjusts CS's sensitivity and m_yaw to preserve both your horizontal cm/360 and your V/H ratio.
