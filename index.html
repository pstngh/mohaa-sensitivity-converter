This converter matches your 360° turn distance between games. The 360° distance is how far you need to move your mouse to complete a full 360° turn in-game.

Game-specific details:

  • MOHAA: Quake 3 engine - sensitivity scales with FOV and aspect ratio. Widescreen resolutions (16:9) result in slower sensitivity compared to 4:3.
  • CS2: Uses fixed yaw value of 0.022. m_pitch is locked but can be simulated.

m_pitch bypass: If you set a custom m_pitch, the converter adjusts sensitivity and m_yaw to simulate the desired vertical feel while keeping horizontal unchanged.

16:9 FOV Equivalents: To match the same cm/360 at 16:9 as 4:3:

  • 4:3 FOV 80 = 16:9 FOV 96.418
  • 5:4 FOV 80 = 16:9 FOV 100.077
