How It Works

This converter matches your 360° turn distance between games. The 360° distance is how far you need to move your mouse to complete a full 360° turn in-game.

Game-specific details:

  • MOHAA: Uses yaw value of ~0.01888 (Quake 3 engine based)
  • CS2: Uses yaw value of 0.022 (Source 2 engine)
  • Formula: cm/360° = (360 × 2.54) ÷ (DPI × sensitivity × yaw)

Note: Resolution affects the aspect ratio but not the sensitivity calculation. FOV is shown for reference but the conversion is based on matching physical mouse movement distance.
