How It Works

This converter matches your 360° turn distance between games. The 360° distance is how far you need to move your mouse to complete a full 360° turn in-game.

Game-specific details:

  • MOHAA: Quake 3 engine - sensitivity scales with FOV and aspect ratio. Widescreen resolutions (16:9) result in slower sensitivity compared to 4:3.
  • CS2: Uses fixed yaw value of 0.022 at FOV 90

Note: Make sure to enter your actual MOHAA resolution and FOV for accurate conversion.
